## What's new in 1.1

**More correct "Sabse sasta"**
- Accessories (case, cable, screen guard "for iPhone 15") are never the same product.
- Other versions ("15 Plus", "Galaxy S24 FE", "Tata Salt Lite", "Popular Plus") are marked *Similar*, not *Same product*.
- Brands with apostrophes (Levi's, Pond's, L'Oréal) now match. "5 litre" = "5 L" = "5 Ltr".
- A listing that doesn't show its size isn't called the same product (the app checks the product page when it can).
- A price far below every other site is flagged instead of winning; out-of-stock items never win.

**Offers you can really use**
- Coupon prices from Myntra, Ajio and Amazon are counted (Ajio used to show the coupon price as the normal price).
- New-user offers, one-app UPI offers (CRED, Jupiter, POP…), "up to ₹X" and coins/points no longer lower the price — no more ₹0 prices.
- My Cards now has UPI apps, memberships (Prime, Flipkart VIP, Myntra Insider, Nykaa Privé) and a "new user" switch.
- Card benefits updated: SBI Cashback cap ₹2,000/cycle, Flipkart Axis & Flipkart SBI caps ₹4,000/quarter, Amazon Pay ICICI 5% only with Prime (3% without), HDFC Millennia on Tata CLiQ, Tata Neu Plus.
- Shows how much is cashback that comes later, and approximate delivery fees.

**Clearer results**
- "Found products, but not this one", "Not sold here" and "Site didn't let us check" are separate. Amazon's empty busy pages are retried.
- Why this match?, sort by price/rating, only same product, hide out of stock, refresh with "checked X min ago".
- Open in the shop's app, price history on Keepa for Amazon, report a wrong price.

**Watchlist**
- Checked every ~4 hours in the background, even with the app closed.
- Target price and back-in-stock alerts; chart with low / average / high and a "good price?" badge.
- No more fake drop alerts after adding a card.

**Also**
- Shared links show "reading the product…" right away; Recent searches remember the exact product.
- Barcode scan, copied-link detection, search suggestions, English / Hinglish / हिंदी, backup & restore, update check, Back button works, optional site logins (beta), privacy promise.
