## What's new in 1.2: quick-delivery apps

**Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, BigBasket, Flipkart Minutes, JioMart and DMart are compared too**
- Set your delivery location once (📍 under the search box): use your current location, or type your pincode or area. It stays on your phone and is sent only to these sites.
- Prices and stock are for the store that delivers to you, with the delivery time (⚡ 10 min).
- New filter: ⚡ Quick delivery. Quick apps add delivery / handling / small-cart fees on small orders, so check the bill.
- Zepto and Instamart only answer real browsers, so the app opens their search page in a hidden in-app browser (one page at a time, no images). You can turn this off in More.
- If a site doesn't deliver to your location, it says so instead of showing a price.
- Watchlist: Blinkit, JioMart and DMart are checked in the background too; the other quick apps when you open the app.
- Amazon Now is inside the Amazon app, so it gets a "check yourself" link. Big Bazaar is now Smart Bazaar, sold online through JioMart.

**Also**
- Location permission is asked only when you tap "Use my current location". Typing a pincode needs no permission.
